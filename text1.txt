Storage Optimization Model

Objective: minimize total storage cost while meeting capacity, performance, availability, and retention requirements.

Minimize:
	C = C_capacity + C_operations + C_transfer + C_downtime + C_compliance

Subject to:
	usable capacity >= demand × (1 + safety margin)
	latency <= service-level target
	availability >= required availability
	retained data >= policy requirement

Decision variables:
- storage tier and media type
- replication or erasure-coding factor
- compression and deduplication levels
- data placement and lifecycle schedules
- reserved versus on-demand capacity

Process:
1. Measure usage, growth, access frequency, latency, and failure rates.
2. Classify data by performance, availability, and retention needs.
3. Place data across hot, warm, cold, and archive tiers.
4. Apply compression, deduplication, and appropriate redundancy.
5. Automate migration, deletion, and capacity scaling.
6. Monitor cost and service objectives; recalculate the model regularly.
